What do the tests check?

Test this phone creates an immediate alert and checks permission, Focus and banner settings. Test automatic reminder sends one message through Campus Pause and Apple—the path future evening reminders use. Test with app closed schedules a test for about three minutes later. Close the app after tapping, then reopen it to check the result.

No banner? Open Notification Center, turn off Focus or Sleep mode, or open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Campus Pause and enable Lock Screen, Notification Center and Banners.

If automatic delivery is not confirmed, renew the saved push connection and test it again.

ONE QUICK SETUP

Add Campus Pause to your Home Screen

On iPhone, this takes a few manual taps in Safari or Chrome. Then open the new icon and enable reminders.

  1. Open your browser’s Share menuIn Safari, tap More or Share. In Chrome, tap Share beside the address bar. Other browsers may place Share inside their menu.
  2. Tap “Add to Home Screen”Scroll the sharing options if you do not see it.
  3. Tap Add, then open the iconLaunch Campus Pause from your Home Screen.
  4. Tap “Enable notifications”Notifications become available after the app opens from that icon.
